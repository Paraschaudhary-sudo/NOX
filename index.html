A LITTLE ABOUT THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

Hey, I’m Paras Chaudhary.

A cinema lover. A curious viewer. The mind behind NOX.

I’ve always been fascinated by cinema — the stories, the characters, the emotions, and the worlds that stay with you long after the screen goes dark.

From legendary movies and binge-worthy web series to anime and timeless cartoons, I love exploring different kinds of stories. There’s always something new to discover, and sometimes, finding what to watch is harder than watching it.

That’s where NOX comes in.

I created NOX with one simple idea: to make discovering your next watch easier. Whether you’re searching for a mind-bending thriller, an underrated movie, a series worth bingeing, or an anime you’ve never heard of, NOX is built to help you find your kind of story.

Because choosing what to watch shouldn’t be a struggle. It should be part of the experience.

Find your next obsession.

— Paras Chaudhary, Founder of NOX
